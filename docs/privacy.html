Privacy Policy

Sentence Explainer — Chrome Extension
Last updated: February 6, 2026

Overview

Sentence Explainer is a Chrome extension that provides AI-powered explanations for highlighted text on webpages. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the extension accesses, how it is used, and what is stored.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our own servers. The extension has no backend, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Data the Extension Accesses

When you highlight text and middle-click, the extension temporarily reads:

  • Selected text — the text you highlighted
  • Surrounding context — the paragraph or block containing the selection
  • Page metadata — the page title, URL, and domain name

This data is used solely to construct a prompt for the AI provider you have configured. It is sent directly from your browser to your chosen API provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or a custom endpoint) and is not sent to or processed by us.

API Keys

Your API key is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's chrome.storage.sync API. This means:

  • The key is stored on your device and synced across your Chrome browsers if you are signed into Chrome
  • The key is only sent to the API provider you selected (OpenAI, Anthropic, or your custom endpoint)
  • We never have access to your API key

Third-Party Services

The extension sends data to third-party AI providers based on your configuration:

  • OpenAI — OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic — Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Custom endpoint — governed by the privacy policy of whichever service you configure

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of your chosen AI provider.

Permissions

  • activeTab — to read selected text and page context only when you trigger an explanation
  • storage — to save your settings (API key, provider, model, custom prompt) locally
  • Host permissions (api.openai.com, api.anthropic.com) — to send requests to AI providers on your behalf

Data Retention

The extension does not retain any page content or explanation results. Selected text and context exist only in memory for the duration of a single request and are discarded immediately after the AI response is displayed.

Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
